WHO WE ARE

Sport. Business.
International relations.

BXP operates at the intersection of professional sport, business development and international relations. We support sports industry stakeholders in identifying the right opportunities, building qualified relationships and turning potential into concrete projects. Our approach is smart, direct and practical: we simplify complexity, open qualified channels and help the different players of the sports industry collaborate clearly and effectively.

BXP acts as a neutral facilitator. We do not operate with conflicts of interest. We create value when clubs, athletes, companies and partners can grow together.

WHAT WE FACILITATE

Five fields.
One ecosystem.

01

Clubs & Sports Organizations

BXP supports clubs and sports organizations in developing international relationships, identifying strategic opportunities and connecting with selected partners. We facilitate projects related to sporting development, commercial growth, international exposure, operational support and institutional positioning.

02

Athletes & Professionals

BXP helps athletes and professional coaches access new contexts, evaluate opportunities and connect with clubs, academies and international sports networks. The goal is to create realistic, coherent and valuable paths, aligned with each professional's profile and ambitions. We facilitate player transfers during market windows, in dialogue with their respective representatives (clubs, agents, family).

03

Companies & Brands

BXP connects companies and brands for commercial partnerships, activations and strategic positioning.

04

Investors & Institutions

BXP facilitates dialogue between institutions and sports organizations, supporting the development of projects related to clubs, academies, events, infrastructure and international expansion.

05

Sports Events

BXP facilitates the creation, organization and development of sports events with an international perspective. We support events designed to connect clubs, athletes, companies, sponsors, institutions and professional stakeholders. Our role can include concept development, partner activation, club and athlete involvement, commercial opportunities, international networking and operational coordination.

Sports events are not just competitions or showcases. They are platforms to generate visibility, relationships and shared value.

HOW WE WORK

Our Process

BXP analyzes the club's starting point, understanding objectives, priorities and growth potential. This phase creates a clear base to build a truly tailored path.

BXP produces a report highlighting strengths, areas for improvement and operational priorities. A concrete tool to help the club make more informed decisions.

BXP builds a personalized strategy, activating only the interventions truly useful to the club. The goal is to generate sporting, organizational and professional value.

BXP accompanies the club throughout the journey, monitoring progress, results and evolution. Growth becomes a continuous, measurable and sustainable process.

OUR MISSION

Why we exist

BXP was created to make the professional sports industry more connected, accessible and efficient. We believe the best opportunities arise when the right people, clubs, companies and institutions are brought together in the right context. Our mission is to facilitate these connections with independence, transparency and strategic clarity.

No conflicts of interest.
Only shared value.

No Conflicts of Interest

BXP operates as an independent facilitator. We do not work to create advantages for one party at the expense of another. We work to identify common ground, align interests and build solutions capable of generating value for all parties involved. This is the foundation of our model.

Why BXP

BXP is designed for an international sports industry that moves fast, demands trust and depends on the quality of relationships. We offer a smart and effective access point for stakeholders who want to navigate the professional sports ecosystem with clarity. We combine international connections, sports industry knowledge and executive capability to facilitate real opportunities.
